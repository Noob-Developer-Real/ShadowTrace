Where you’ll spend your time

Cases, Activity and Protection each have their own page in the sidebar. This is what each one is for.

Review cases

A case collects the events that looked wrong, in the order they happened. Read it through, write down what you decided, then close it or flag it for follow-up.

  • Findings sorted by severity, critical first
  • A timeline of every event in the case
  • A history of who reviewed it and what they said

Follow the record

Pick a record and see who touched it and where it went afterwards. Lineage shows the whole path, from the first access to wherever the data ended up.

  • Every hop between the source and the destination
  • Destinations nobody expected are marked
  • Open the case a record belongs to from the same page

Check protection

The Protection page lists the transfers that were stopped and the reason each one was stopped. If a number jumps, you’ll see it here first.

  • Blocked transfers in a single log
  • The reason recorded for each block
  • Totals at the top, so a spike is easy to spot

From first event to decision

Every case goes through the same three steps.

  1. Detect

    Access that doesn’t match an account’s normal pattern is flagged. Related events are grouped into one case and given a severity.

  2. Review

    An analyst reads the findings and the timeline, then records what they decided and why.

  3. Follow up

    If something needs a second look, it stays on the case as a follow-up until someone closes it.

Who uses it

Your role is set per workspace, so you can be an analyst in one and an admin in another.

Common questions

What is a case?

A group of related access events that looked unusual. Reviewing them together means one decision per case instead of one per event.

Who can see my workspace?

Only its members. After you sign in you’ll see the workspaces you belong to, and nothing else.

Is the demo using real data?

No. The demo identities (Northwind Commerce and Harbor Supply) and everything inside them are synthetic.
